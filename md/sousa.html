Protein + Hydration Recovery Drink Mix

Experience the ultimate dual-action recovery with our premium Protein + Hydration formula. Designed to support your body from the inside out, this versatile blend combines high-quality nutrition with essential hydration in one convenient serving. Whether you are finishing a high-intensity training session, powering through a busy day at work, or simply relaxing, our mix provides a smooth and delicious way to refuel and recover.

Each serving is meticulously formulated to deliver optimal results:

  • High-Quality Protein: 20g of premium whey protein to support muscle recovery and growth.
  • Advanced Hydration: 1500+mg of electrolytes to replenish essential minerals lost during activity.
  • Essential Vitamins: A powerful blend of Vitamins C, B3, B6, and B12 to support energy metabolism and overall wellness.

Our convenient, single-serve packets ensure you can enjoy a creamy, satisfying boost whenever and wherever you need it. Simply mix and enjoy the perfect balance of performance and flavor.

Each box contains 14 packets/servings.
